Seeded development example, not physical validation. Six panels show the stored stress [MPa], extension [mm] and reaction [N] for the control (circles) and the half-modulus artifact (squares). Panels (a) to (c): prescribed force, 1000 N. Panels (d) to (f): prescribed displacement, 0.025 mm. The dashed line is the independent specification reference. A filled marker means the panel's check passes against that reference; a hollow marker means it fails. Under prescribed force only the extension check detects the fault; under prescribed displacement the stress and reaction checks detect the fault. Both half-modulus artifacts fail the full response tuple. Uniform axial bar, 100 mm long, 20 mm² section; one deterministic artifact per point, so there are no error bars. Values: matrix.md.
