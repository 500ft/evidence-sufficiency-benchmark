# Conditional LE1 source and rights audit

LE1 was not run or counted. The [NAFEMS P18 publication page](https://www.nafems.org/publications/resource_center/p18/) identifies the standard elliptic-membrane benchmark, but the full specification is offered through its subscription or purchase route. No rights to redistribute that document, deck or figure were established here, and no purchase was authorized.

The author-maintained [CoFEA model page](https://cofea.readthedocs.io/en/latest/benchmarks/004-eliptic-membrane/model.html) identifies a plane-stress membrane target at D, material modulus and Poisson ratio, symmetry edges and a pressure load. Its [results page](https://cofea.readthedocs.io/en/latest/benchmarks/004-eliptic-membrane/results.html) gives mesh-dependent stresses. The [CoFEA repository](https://github.com/spolanski/CoFEA/tree/0886c7015330105054dfa419d3cc2e3ee0ddabf7/benchmarks/04-eliptic-membrane) carries a [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license](https://github.com/spolanski/CoFEA/blob/0886c7015330105054dfa419d3cc2e3ee0ddabf7/LICENSE.md) and links a third-party source. That repository license does not establish rights in the underlying NAFEMS specification or the linked third-party material. The inspected text does not pin a complete point-D stress extraction/averaging convention or a separately converged, independent answer. Software availability and the displayed target therefore do not qualify LE1. No deck, image, source text or published target was copied into a case, and no tolerance was enlarged to match one.

A future LE1 qualification would need the complete authorized primary geometry and thickness, plane-stress model, material, pressure and symmetry specification, exact point D/component and extraction rule, display precision, source rights, a separate refinement solve and scoped Claude technical review. This conditional exclusion does not change the provisional coverage target.
